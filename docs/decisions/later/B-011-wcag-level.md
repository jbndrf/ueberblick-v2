# B-011 Conformance claim: WCAG 2.1 AA (BITV 2.0 / EN 301 549); WCAG 2.2 AA stays the design goal
priority: low      status: provisional
date: 2026-09-30   lead: b   work packages: WP-554   touches: docs/09, accessibility statement

## Context
docs/09 names WCAG 2.2 AA. The binding standard for German public bodies is BITV 2.0, which references EN 301 549 v3.2.1 and thereby WCAG 2.1 AA. The accessibility statement must claim conformance against the binding standard.

## Options
- A) Claim and self-check WCAG 2.1 AA (legally binding); keep 2.2 AA as the design goal and check its additional criteria as a stretch.
- B) Claim 2.2 AA (stricter than required; statement terminology then diverges from BITV).

## Chosen (provisional)
A. The statement matches the law; nothing designed for 2.2 is lost. Seam: the self-check lists the 2.2-only criteria separately, so switching the claim is a document change when EN 301 549 moves to 2.2.

## Cost to change later
now: low · after the build: low.
