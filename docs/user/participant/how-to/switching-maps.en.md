# Switching maps: Map layers

Open the **Map layers** sheet with **Layers** (bottom bar on a phone, header on a computer). It sits at the left edge, and the map stays visible and usable.

- **Base maps:** Exactly one is active, for example streets or aerial photo. Tap another and the map changes at once.
- **Overlays:** Each overlay, for example parcels, has a switch. Any number can be on; they sit above the base map and under your features.
- **What you see:** Your project decides which layers exist and which of them your role may see. You see only yours. The first time you open the map, the project's start selection is on.
- **Remembered:** Your choice belongs to you, not to the device: it stays after the app closes and applies on another device where you sign in. Without a connection the device keeps it and syncs it later.
- **No layers:** If the project has no layers, the sheet says "No map layers configured for this project"; the map then shows OpenStreetMap. A project with only overlay layers and no base map also gets OpenStreetMap underneath. The tiles come through the project's server, not straight from OpenStreetMap.
- **Credit:** The source of the base map is credited at the bottom right of the map, for example the OpenStreetMap street map.
- **Offline:** Tiles from the network are not available without a connection; your features stay visible.
- **Privacy:** Map tiles load through your project's server, not from a third-party provider; your device never contacts a map provider.
