# The project chat

With the **Project chat** you write to everyone in the project. It is a tool: if it is not under **Tools**, your role does not have it, or it is only offered and not switched on yet. Switch it on yourself then: **Settings**, tab **Options**, **Advanced features**, switch **Project chat**.

## Reading and writing
1. Open **Tools** and tap **Project chat**. The chat window stands on the right, full width on a phone. The map below stays usable, and the chat stays open when you open **Layers**, **Filters**, **Tools** or **Settings**; only the **+** closes it.
2. Messages run from the top (old) to the bottom (new). Consecutive messages by the same person that are less than five minutes apart stand under one line with name and time. Your own messages stand on the right and are called "You"; a person whose name your device does not know is called "Unknown". If nothing has been written yet, it says "No messages yet. Be the first to say hi.".
3. Type in the field "Message…" and send with Enter (or the **Send** button). Shift+Enter or Alt+Enter starts a new line.
4. Close the window with **Close** or the Escape key.

While you read, the chat stays where you are: if you have scrolled up, a new message does not jump to the bottom. If you are at the bottom, it follows the new messages. You always see your own message.

## Mentioning someone
- With the **Mention someone** list you put a person into the text. In the message they appear as a name with @; when they are mentioned, their name has a yellow background. The list shows everyone in the project who has the chat, by name, also people who have not written yet. It comes with the messages and stays on your device, so it is there without a connection too (once the chat has loaded once); a new person appears with the next update. If it is empty it says "No participants".
- You mention a feature in its feature sheet: top right the menu **More actions**, there **Mention in chat**. The chat opens and the mention of the feature is in the text field; nothing is sent yet. In the message the feature appears with # and its name. A tap on it opens the feature sheet, and the chat stays open. The menu is missing while a form or a quick edit is open, and for roles without the chat.

## Unread
On the **Tools** icon (bottom bar, header on a computer) and on the **Project chat** tile a red counter shows how many unread messages mention you (from 100 `99+`). If there are only unread messages without a mention, it is a grey dot (read aloud as "Unread"). When you open the chat everything counts as read; the server keeps this by the order of the messages, not by your device's clock, and it applies on all your devices.

## Without a connection
The chat works on this device. A message you send without a connection stands in the chat at once with "Sending …" and goes out as soon as the device is online again. If the server refuses it, it says "Not sent…" with the reason, plus **Send again** and **Discard**. New messages from others appear after a few seconds without you doing anything.

Good to know:
- Messages cannot be edited or deleted, and there is no notification besides the counter.
- If a person is deleted from the project, their messages disappear, and a mention of them or of a deleted feature then reads `[removed]` in the text.
- If your role changes while the chat is open, it says "Project chat is not available for your role.".
