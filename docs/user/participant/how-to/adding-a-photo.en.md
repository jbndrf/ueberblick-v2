# Adding a photo

Some forms have a photo field. It shows two buttons.

1. Tap **Take photo** to open the camera, or **Choose photo** to pick an image from your device.
2. Wait a moment. The photo is made smaller on your device first; the message "Processing photo …" turns into "Photo added" with the file name, and a preview appears.
3. To add another photo, tap one of the buttons again. Each photo is added to the ones already there, until the maximum the project set for this field (10 unless the project says otherwise); then the buttons are off and a message says so.
4. **Choose photo** lets you pick several photos at once when the field holds more than one. They are added one after another, in the order you picked them. If you pick more than still fit, the first ones are added and the rest are left out. **Take photo** takes one picture at a time.

Good to know:
- Photos work without a connection. They are saved on your device and sent later with the task.
- Your location is **not** stored with the photo, unless the project allows it for this field.
- The photo is saved as a JPEG and reduced to a size suitable for the project.
- If a photo cannot be processed, a message says so; try another image.
- Everything works with the keyboard: press Tab to reach the buttons and Enter or Space to open the picker.
- A field can hold several photos or files. The project sets the maximum (10 unless the project says otherwise) and the allowed file types. A file over the maximum, or of a type the field does not allow, is refused on that field.
