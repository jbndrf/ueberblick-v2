# Filling in a task

Open a task from the feature sheet or with the plus on the map. The form opens in the same window as the feature sheet.

1. Fill in the fields from top to bottom. Fields that do not fit the situation are hidden and appear on their own once they are needed. Hidden fields are not saved.
2. Prefilled values (for example the current state) can be changed at any time. Your change stays.
3. Required fields carry a red star. Photos, positions and similar inputs are taken with the tool inside the field.
4. If the form has several pages, tap **Next** at the bottom. **Back** keeps everything you entered.
5. Tap **Submit** (or **Next**). If something is wrong, a list of problems appears at the top. Tapping an entry jumps to the field.

The button at the bottom is large and within thumb reach. Everything also works with the keyboard.

## Drafts, offline and refusals

- **Drafts:** your entries are saved as a draft on this device about a second after you stop typing, and when you close the sheet, so even if the phone closes the app they are kept. Open the same task again and your entries are back ("Your draft was restored"). Submitting removes the draft.
- **After you submit:** As soon as the change is stored on the device, the form closes, online or offline; the draft is gone. At the feature, "Not uploaded yet: n." shows until the server has accepted the change.
- **Not possible:** If your device already refuses the task, the form stays open with your entries and a red box "Not possible" gives the reason; nothing was saved. **Dismiss** hides the box.
- **Refused:** If the server refuses the task later, the feature shows "Refused" with the reason, **Send again** and **Discard**. The same appears under Settings, Profile, "Pending changes" (see [When a run is refused](../refused-runs.en.md)).
