# Adding a new object

1. Tap **+** at the bottom (on a computer, at the top in the header). You see one button per kind of object your role may create (named after the task).
2. Tap the kind of object.
3. If the kind has a point on the map, a **red pin** appears in the middle of the map and a red button with the task name at the bottom. Drag the pin to the exact spot, or tap the map to put the pin there; a dashed line joins the red button to the pin. Then tap the red button. The **x** next to it cancels.
4. The form opens with that spot as its location. If the kind has no location, the form opens right after step 2.

**Chain to parent element:** If the kind belongs to a parent object (for example a tree to an area) and the link is not required, a chain button sits at the top right while you place the pin. Tap it: the hint "Tap the parent element" appears and every possible parent in the map view gets a ring. Tap a ring: the pin jumps onto the parent, a dashed line joins them, and the form opens with the parent linked. A second tap on the chain button removes the link; you then create the object on its own.

**Kinds that sit at their parent** (for example a measure at a tree) have no location of their own and no pin. After you tap the kind, the hint and the rings appear at once; tapping the parent opens the form with the parent linked. **Cancel** at the bottom cancels. From the object itself you create such children with "New {name}", which needs no tap on the map.

Kinds that are a line or an area are drawn on the map instead of placing a pin: tap the points, then **Done**; the form opens with the shape. See [Drawing a line or an area on the map](drawing-a-geometry.en.md).

While you place the pin, the bottom bar is hidden. If you have reached the number of entries your role may create, the buttons are greyed out and a hint says to delete an entry first. Tapping beside the picker closes it (and Recent, if open).

## Recent
With Recent open, the picker moves to the left half.
Tap **+** a second time (on a computer, the clock) to open **Recent**: the 50 objects changed last. Tapping a row flies to the object and opens it. Objects without a location (**no location**) are reachable only here and through the list.

Each row shows the object type's icon or colour (as on the map), the type, what was done last (for example "3 fields updated", "Location updated", "Stage change: Review"), the object's name, how long ago, and its stage. That line appears a moment after the list opens.
