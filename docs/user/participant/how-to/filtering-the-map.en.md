# Map content: showing and hiding features

The **Map content** sheet sits at the left edge of the map, and the map stays visible and usable. Open it with **Filters** in the bottom bar (on a computer, in the header).

- **One card per feature type:** Every feature type your device holds features of appears as a card with the number of its features. The switch hides or shows all features of that type on the map. The map follows at once.
- **Hide single stages:** If a feature type has a process (for example Captured, Checked, Done), an arrow on the left unfolds one switch per stage with its count. That lets you hide, for example, all finished features. Turn the type off and every stage is off; then turn one stage on and the type comes back with only that stage. Turn the last stage off and the type goes off too. A stage with no features stays in the list (count 0), so you can always turn it back on.
- **Icon or dot:** Before every type and stage stands the icon or coloured dot the map draws it with; your project sets it. With none, it is a plain dot. This holds for every feature type the map shows, not only that of the start view: the map and the sheet show the same icon. A new project version shows the next time the map is opened.
- **Stage or another property:** When the feature type has a property the map tells its features apart by (for example the category of a report), the arrow unfolds one switch per value of that property; otherwise one per stage. Everything else works the same.
- **No place on the map:** Feature types without a place on the map (for example lookup lists, with neither a place of their own nor a seat at their parent's place) are not listed.
- **Children at their parent's place (for example Inspection, Measure):** They are listed as cards of their own, with the count and their stages (for example Open, Overdue), as soon as the view's map draws them. Hiding a type or a stage hides it everywhere: its markers, its stacks and its share of the number on the parent. The parent shows that number (open children) for the children that are visible right now only. With the parent hidden, the visible children sit at its place; with both on, the parent is on top and the children behind it (tap to open them). Which children the map shows, and whether only open ones, is set by your project's view through its layers and their filters.
- **Views (tool):** The **Views** tab belongs to the tool *Views*. It shows when your role offers it and you switch it on under Settings, Advanced features, Filter (**Manage tabs**, top right of the sheet, leads there); switched off, your own views are not available. The filters your project prepared for your role stay under *From the project* for everyone in the role, even with the tool off; if there are none, there is no tab. The tab shows **Default**, your own views and *From the project*. A chosen view replaces the switches while it is on; a note at the top of the Simple tab names it, the switches are locked, and **Turn view off** (or **Default**) brings them back. Further tabs (for example Cluster) come the same way.
- **Build your own view:** **New view** creates a view and turns it on; the filter editor appears under it. Under **Filter fields** pick the fields (the feature type shown below each): text *Contains…*, number *Min* and *Max*, date *From* and *To*, choice fields *Any value*. Under **Add other condition** there are **Stage**, **Created date**, **Updated date** (older than, newer than, between; the last day counts) and **Created by** (shown by the person's name). All conditions must match; an empty condition does not count. Field and stage conditions apply to their feature type only, date and creator to all. The map follows every change at once, also offline. The **X** on a condition removes it. A condition the editor cannot show as a row (for example one written by hand) stays as a **Rule** and is kept when you save.
- **Remembered view:** Your views and the one that is active follow you to every device (personal settings). So does the last chosen tab.
- **Cluster:** The map groups features that lie close together. When the **Cluster** feature is on under Settings, Advanced features (if your project offers it), the sheet shows the **Cluster** tab. The **Uncluster** switch shows the features in the current map view one by one while there are at most as many as **Up to** says (default 500, whole numbers from 1; an invalid entry is ignored). With more, they stay grouped, and the line below says how many are in view. The switch and the number belong to you, follow you to other devices and hold offline, with the sheet closed and after a restart.
- **Remembered:** Your switches in **Simple** belong to your account: they survive a reload and follow you to another device (offline too, they are sent later). Without switches of your own, the project's first-start setting applies.
- **Numbers:** The numbers count all features on your device, hidden ones included.
