# Measuring a distance or an area

The **Measure** tool on the map tells you how long a path is or how large an area is. It only measures. It never changes a feature.

> Note: Your project may offer **Measure** without switching it on. Then switch it on yourself: **Settings**, tab **Options**, **Advanced features**, switch **Measure**. Only then is the tool under **Tools**.

1. On the map, open **Tools** and tap **Measure**. The panel shows **Distance** and **Area**; pick one.
2. Tap each point on the map, or walk to it and tap **Add my position** (your device asks for permission the first time).
3. The result appears at once. For a distance you see the total and the last leg. For an area you see the area and its perimeter. A distance needs two points, an area three.
4. **Undo** removes the last point. **Clear** removes all of them and the line or area on the map.

Good to know:
- Lengths are in metres or kilometres, areas in square metres, hectares or square kilometres.
- If the outline of an area crosses itself, the panel says so and does not measure. Undo the last point or clear.
- The values are the same ones the project uses for its own features.
- Measuring works without a connection.
- Measure appears under **Tools** while the map is showing. When the list stands in for the map (the map could not be drawn, or you switched to the list with **List**), it is not offered: there is nothing to tap. Switch back to the map and it is there again.
- The panel sits at the bottom and does not dim the map: you can keep tapping the map above it. **Close** (the X) closes the panel and removes the line from the map.
- A tap on a feature opens the feature, it does not add a point. Tap the empty map between features.
